Website & digital presence

Your website is the front door to your business — and the first source of data about your customers. We build it to do both jobs well.

We design and build fast, accessible marketing sites and web applications, and we instrument them properly from the start: clean event tracking, consent handling and analytics that flow straight into your data platform instead of living in a silo.

Outcome

Data engineering

Reliable pipelines that bring data from every system you run into one clean, well-modeled source of truth.

We connect your SaaS tools, databases, files and APIs; land the data in a modern warehouse or lakehouse; and transform it into tested, documented models that analytics and machine learning can build on with confidence.

Outcome

Fresh, trustworthy data in one place, on a schedule you can rely on.

What we deliver
  • Ingestion from SaaS apps, databases, APIs and files
  • Batch and real-time streaming pipelines
  • Warehouse or lakehouse design and setup
  • Version-controlled transformation and data modeling
  • Automated data quality tests, documentation and lineage
  • Orchestration, scheduling and failure alerting
Typical tooling
  • Snowflake
  • BigQuery
  • Databricks
  • PostgreSQL
  • dbt
  • Airflow
  • Fivetran
  • Airbyte
  • Kafka

Machine learning & AI

Practical machine learning and AI that moves beyond the pilot and into daily operations.

We start with the business decision, not the algorithm. Then we build models on your governed data, deploy them into the tools your teams already use, and monitor them so they stay accurate as conditions change.
