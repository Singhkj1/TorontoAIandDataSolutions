How we work

A clear path from first call to production — and beyond.

A staged, transparent process with something tangible at every step. You always know what we're building, why, and what it will cost.

The process

Five phases. No black boxes.

  1. 01

    Discover typically 1–2 weeks

    We get to know your business, your systems and your data — and agree what success looks like in business terms, not technical ones.

    • Stakeholder interviews and goal setting
    • Audit of current systems, data sources and tooling
    • Quick-win and risk assessment
    You receive

    A discovery report and a prioritized roadmap.

  2. 02

    Architect

    We design the target stack end to end — how data enters, where it lives, how it's modeled, and how it reaches people and products.

    • Target architecture across all relevant layers
    • Tooling recommendations that fit your scale and budget
    • Security, access and compliance model
    You receive

    An architecture blueprint and a scoped proposal with timeline and cost.

  3. 03

    Build

    We deliver in short increments, so you see working software early and can steer as priorities change.

    • Two-week delivery cycles with live demos
    • Infrastructure as code, automated tests and code review
    • Weekly progress updates in plain language
    You receive

    Production-ready systems, delivered piece by piece.

  4. 04

    Launch & hand over

    We take each component live carefully, then make sure your team can own and operate it with confidence.

    • Staged go-live with rollback plans
    • Documentation and operational runbooks
    • Hands-on training for your team
    You receive

    Live systems, full documentation and a team that knows how to use them.

  5. 05

    Run & improve

    Data platforms are never finished. We can stay on to keep everything healthy and keep extending what it can do.

    • Monitoring, alerting and incident response
    • Regular reviews of performance, cost and usage
    • An ongoing roadmap of improvements
    You receive

    Reliable operations and a platform that keeps getting better.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do we have to hire you for the whole stack?

No. Many clients start with a single layer — often a reporting overhaul or a data warehouse — and expand from there. Because we think about the full stack, whatever we build first is designed to fit with what comes next.

Who owns the code, data and infrastructure?

You do. We work in your cloud accounts and your code repositories from day one, and everything is documented. If you ever decide to move on, there's nothing to untangle.

Which cloud platforms and tools do you work with?

We work across AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud, and with widely used tools such as Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks, dbt, Power BI and Looker. If you already have investments in place, we'll build on them rather than replace them for the sake of it.

Can you work alongside our in-house team?

Yes. We regularly embed with internal engineering, data and marketing teams — filling skill gaps, adding capacity, and leaving your people more capable than when we started.

How is pricing structured?

After discovery we provide a scoped proposal. Well-defined projects are usually quoted at a fixed price; ongoing operations and improvement work is typically a monthly retainer. You'll always know the cost before work begins.

What happens after launch?

Your choice. We can stay on to run, monitor and improve the platform under a managed service, or hand it over to your team with documentation, runbooks and training.
