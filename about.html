About us

We build the whole stack because the gaps are where projects fail.

NifyDataSolutions is a data and digital infrastructure partner. We design, build and run the full stack — website, pipelines, analytics, machine learning and the cloud underneath — for organizations that want results, not a vendor-management project.

Why we exist

The problem with stitching it together.

Building a modern data capability usually means hiring a web agency, a data engineering firm, an analytics vendor and a machine-learning specialist — and then becoming the integrator yourself.

Each partner may do good work inside their own layer. But the website's tracking doesn't match the warehouse. The warehouse wasn't modeled for the dashboards. And the machine-learning pilot never reaches production, because nobody owns the path from raw data to a deployed model.

NifyDataSolutions exists to close those gaps. We take responsibility for the whole system, so every layer is designed with the next one in mind — and there's one team to call when something needs to change.

Our mission

Give every organization the data infrastructure of a tech company — without having to become one.

What we believe

The principles behind our work.
